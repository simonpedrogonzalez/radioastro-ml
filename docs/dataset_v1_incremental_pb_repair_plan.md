# Incremental dataset-v1 completion, level expansion, and PB-mask repair

## Starting point

- Resume **`collect/experiments/dataset_v1_20260921_all_psf`**, not a new timestamped directory. Its `dataset.json` contains 1,171 retained samples from 131 sources; eight requested corruption variants failed.
- Its precursor is **`collect/experiments/extracted_thermal_simulation_comparison_20260909T005739`**: 131 completed sources and two recorded failures (`1111+199`, `2257-364`). The 12 newly moved MSs are under `collect/extracted/<id>/<id>/<id>.ms` but have no precursor entry yet.
- An audit of the retained dirty/CLEAN/residual FITS finds **25 circular-support sources, covering 224 completed samples** (19 train, 3 validation, 3 test sources). Do not hard-code these IDs; detect support from the retained images so interrupted repairs resume correctly.
- Expand requested `SNR_corr` levels from `(10, 30, 50, 100)` to **`(5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 40, 50, 100)`** for both amplitude and phase. That is one baseline plus 16 corruption variants per fully completed source. Each old source needs only the eight new level/type combinations unless one of its old variants is still missing or needs PB repair.
- The two-MS PB test in [the earlier plan](primary_beam_support_recovery_plan.md) found that `pblimit=-0.1` filled the circular source's previously masked pixels; the square control's four FITS products were pixel-identical. CASA says the negative sign removes the output T/F PB mask; `-0.1` is not a universal normalization-off switch. [CASA `tclean` documentation](https://casadocs.readthedocs.io/en/stable/api/tt/casatasks.imaging.tclean.html#pblimit).

## Label compatibility

The existing dataset's `dataset.json` already assigns IDs 0–8. **Never renumber them by enumerating the expanded, sorted level list.** Keep `not_corrupted=0`; amplitude levels 10/30/50/100 at IDs 1/2/3/4; and phase levels 10/30/50/100 at IDs 5/6/7/8. Assign IDs 9/10/11/12 to `amp_snr_5/15/20/40` and 13/14/15/16 to `phase_snr_5/15/20/40`. The preprocessing loader's separate three-class `(none, amp, phase)` criterion and its numeric corruption-level metadata need no change.

Its existing `main()` already skips verified completed entries and leaves recorded partial failures alone. 2. Compare old/new image values on formerly valid circular pixels, verify intended metric-region changes, and inspect the expanded report grid.
3. After continuation, audit every retained source/variant for common full-square support, valid four-product FITS/WCS/integrity, unique indexed sample IDs, stable old labels, correct new labels/levels, unchanged source splits, and explicit remaining thermal/corruption failures. A second identical run must not redo completed MSs or samples; it may retry still-missing corruption variants.

This plan does **not** rebuild already-completed square samples at old levels, alter the two previously failed thermal sources, or change the source-flux definition. It does change the valid pixel population—and therefore potentially the recorded image metrics—for repaired circular samples. The expanded level distribution also changes the training population; model baselines trained on the old dataset must be rerun before comparison.
